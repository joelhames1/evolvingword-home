    About

    Evolving Word is where we make things together.

    It started with a shirt for a trip and turned into a little studio: a place where family and friends dream something up, argue happily about the details, and then actually make it.

    Everything here is built with AI as a collaborator: the words, the pictures, even this room. The rain is code. So is the music on the tape deck.

    Credits

    Everything you see and hear is made in your browser as you watch: no photos, no 3D models, no audio files. The rain, the city, the billboard and the music on the tape are all code.

    The billboard runs Richard Dawkins' "weasel program" from The Blind Watchmaker (1986): random letters, copied with mistakes, the best copy kept, generation after generation, until they spell something.

    Built with three.js. Type: Archivo Black, VT323, Caveat and Space Mono (SIL Open Font License). Made with Claude.
